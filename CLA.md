# Contributor License Agreements

Before we can accept your contribution, you must sign a Contributor License Agreement ("CLA") with CybeDefend SAS:

- the **Individual Contributor License Agreement** below, if you contribute on your own behalf;
- the **Corporate Contributor License Agreement** below as well, if you contribute on behalf of your employer, or if your employer has rights to intellectual property that you create. Your employer signs it once and lists its designated employees in Schedule A.

These agreements are adapted from the Apache Software Foundation's Individual Contributor License Agreement V2.2 and Software Grant and Corporate Contributor License Agreement. They are not endorsed by the Apache Software Foundation.

## How to sign

**Individual CLA.** When you open your first pull request, the CLA Assistant bot asks you to sign. Sign by posting this exact comment on the pull request:

```
I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA
```

The bot records your signature against your GitHub account. You sign once: it covers all your later pull requests.

**Corporate CLA.** Fill in the Corporate CLA below, including Schedule A, have it signed by a person authorised to bind the Corporation, and send it to **opensource@cybedefend.com**. Each designated employee also signs the Individual CLA on their pull request.
